InnoFlow - Plant Refuel Log

Designed to streamline and record plant refueling operations across construction sites. InnoFlow replaces manual paper dockets and disconnected spreadsheets with 3 integrated core functional modules:

01

Site, Equipment & Fuel Price Data Management

Centralized master registries for project codes, plant equipment specs, and time-bound daily oil prices.

Section 2.1 · Master Data Governance

Site, Equipment & Fuel Price Data Management

Centralized master data governance ensures consistent project coding, standardized plant/equipment profiles, and accurate time-bound daily oil pricing across every site delivery log.

Section 2.3 · Real-Time Analytics & Cost Reporting

Daily & Monthly Usage Dashboard

Validated delivery records flow automatically into two real-time analytical views—filterable by Refuel Date, Project Code, and Management Company.

Daily Usage

Volume (Litres)

Tracks daily fuel consumption (Litres) broken down by site and by plant (Type No., Plant Type, Description, Brand, Model, Total Usage (Litre)) with automatic daily aggregation.

Monthly Cost

Expenditure (HK$)

Calculates monthly fuel expenditure (HK$) by site per month based on the effective Unit Rate ($HK/litre) and aggregated plant consumption volumes.

Section 3 · Phase 2 Roadmap

Post Go-Live Enhancement Initiatives

Building upon validated fuel delivery records, the upcoming enhancement introduces Plant Operation Hours & Loading Tracking to unlock granular equipment efficiency intelligence and sharper project cost estimation.

Core Enhancement Initiative

Plant Operation Hours & Loading Tracking

Pair refuel quantities with actual equipment running hours and operational load profiles across every site.

Post Go-Live Roadmap

1. Field Data Capture

Enable project teams to log daily plant operation hours and workload/loading conditions directly alongside refuel logs.

2. Actual Fuel Consumption Calculation

Combine actual fuel volume consumed with recorded operating hours to compute true L/hr consumption rates per plant.

3. Precise Cost Control & Anomaly Detection

Benchmark actual vs. standard consumption rates to flag abnormal fuel usage, excessive idling waste, or potential leakage early.

4. Data-Driven Tendering Estimation

Build historical plant fuel and loading benchmarks to support more accurate, evidence-backed cost estimation for future project tenders.
